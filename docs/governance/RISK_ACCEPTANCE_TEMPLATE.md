Specify the exact scope, monitoring/alert thresholds, kill switch, incident owner, review cadence, remediation tasks, and exit criteria.

The following are not acceptable through this template: committed credentials; storage or logging of OTP, PIN, CVV, or full card data; bypassed authentication/object authorization/consent; unencrypted restricted financial data; fabricated compliance or recovery evidence; or an open critical exploitable vulnerability.
